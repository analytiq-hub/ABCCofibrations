# References on cofibration categories

Candidate references for this formalization. They are not an audit of correspondence
with [arXiv:math/0610009v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0610009v4), and a listing
here does not mean the work has been checked against the Lean development.

The citing list was taken from Semantic Scholar for `arXiv:math/0610009` on
2026-11-07 (69 records). Where that index stored a publisher placeholder, the
title below is the arXiv title. Duplicate index records of one preprint are
collapsed. An index citation is evidence that the record's reference list
points here; it is not by itself evidence that the work uses the ABC axioms.

Baues cofibration categories, Waldhausen categories, and categories of
cofibrant objects are related axiomatizations. They are not the same as
Anderson–Brown–Cisinski cofibration categories. Chapter 2 of the source paper
is the comparison.

## The source of this repository

- Andrei Rădulescu-Banu, *Cofibrations in Homotopy Theory*,
  [arXiv:math/0610009v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0610009v4) (2009 revision;
  first posted 2006). Definition and theorem numbers in this repository refer
  to this version.

## Sources of the axioms

These are the accounts named in the introduction of the source paper, together
with the standard neighboring axiom systems.

- Daniel G. Quillen, *Homotopical Algebra*, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 43,
  Springer, 1967.
  [doi:10.1007/BFb0097438](https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0097438).
- Kenneth S. Brown, *Abstract homotopy theory and generalized sheaf cohomology*,
  Transactions of the American Mathematical Society 186 (1973), 419–458.
  [doi:10.1090/S0002-9947-1973-0341469-9](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1973-0341469-9).
  Categories of fibrant objects; the dual is the category of cofibrant objects.
- Donald W. Anderson, *Fibrations and geometric realizations*, Bulletin of the
  American Mathematical Society 84 (1978), 765–788.
  [doi:10.1090/S0002-9904-1978-14512-1](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9904-1978-14512-1).
  The source paper renames Anderson's left homotopical categories as ABC
  cofibration categories, with axioms slightly more general than Anderson's.
- Denis-Charles Cisinski, *Images directes cohomologiques dans les catégories
  de modèles*, Annales mathématiques Blaise Pascal 10 (2003), no. 2, 195–244.
  [doi:10.5802/ambp.174](https://doi.org/10.5802/ambp.174).
- Denis-Charles Cisinski, *Les préfaisceaux comme modèles des types
  d'homotopie*, Astérisque 308, Société Mathématique de France, 2006.
- Denis-Charles Cisinski, *Catégories dérivables*, Bulletin de la Société
  Mathématique de France 138 (2010), no. 3, 317–393.
  [doi:10.24033/bsmf.2592](https://doi.org/10.24033/bsmf.2592).
- Hans Joachim Baues, *Algebraic Homotopy*, Cambridge Studies in Advanced
  Mathematics 15, Cambridge University Press, 1989. Baues' cofibration-category
  axioms.
- Friedhelm Waldhausen, *Algebraic K-theory of spaces*, in *Algebraic and
  Geometric Topology*, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 1126, Springer, 1985,
  318–419. Waldhausen categories.
- Alex Heller, *Homotopy theories*, Memoirs of the American Mathematical
  Society 71 (1988), no. 383.
  [doi:10.1090/memo/0383](https://doi.org/10.1090/memo/0383).
  Heller derivators, the target of the construction in the source paper.

Accounts of homotopy colimits cited by the source paper for comparison:

- Philip S. Hirschhorn, *Model Categories and Their Localizations*,
  Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 99, American Mathematical Society, 2003.
- Mark Hovey, *Model Categories*, Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 63,
  American Mathematical Society, 1999.
- William G. Dwyer, Philip S. Hirschhorn, Daniel M. Kan, and Jeffrey H. Smith,
  *Homotopy Limit Functors on Model Categories and Homotopical Categories*,
  Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 113, American Mathematical Society, 2004.

A short guide to the several axiom systems, with further pointers, is the
nLab page [cofibration category](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/cofibration+category).
